Six months in, still doing it by hand.

SourPuss NH Bakery started in Lee, New Hampshire, boiling and baking bagels the traditional way — no shortcuts, no par-baking, no steam ovens. Every batch is shaped, boiled, and topped by hand before it goes to Nashua, Lee, Somersworth, or Dover.

The menu stays small on purpose: ten reliable everyday flavors, plus a short rotating list that changes weekly. It keeps every batch fresh and lets new flavors get tried out often.

— Elizabeth Meeker, Owner
