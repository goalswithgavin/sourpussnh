About SourPuss

Made by hand, in a real kitchen

SourPuss NH Bakery is a licensed cottage food business run by Elizabeth Meeker out of a home kitchen in Lee, New Hampshire. Every bagel is naturally leavened, hand-shaped, boiled, and baked in small batches — no shortcuts, no mixes, just the long way of doing it because it's the way that actually works.

We don't have a storefront. We have a weekly route — Nashua on Tuesdays, Lee pickup on Thursdays, Somersworth and Dover Friday mornings — and a list of flavors that ranges from a proper plain bagel to whatever specialty flavor is in the oven that week.

Don't take our word for it. Just taste them.
